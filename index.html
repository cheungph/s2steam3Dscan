How Do Our Devices Calculate Depth (z)?

2 Ways to Solve z

Mechanism A: LiDAR Scanner

(Light Bounce)

• Shoots invisible light pulses at the wall and times how fast they bounce back.

Depth (z) =
Speed of light × Time (t) 2
(divide by 2:
there & back!)
Note: Needs LiDAR Pro hardware

Mechanism B: Video-to-3D AI

(Photogrammetry)

• Works on ANY phone or iPad! Walk while filming → AI spots the SAME corner from 2+ angles → solves a TRIANGLE for (x, y, z).

Engineer's Challenge: WHY Do These 5 Rules Exist?

Use (x, y, z) coordinates, Light Bounce, or Triangles to think of the math reason first! Click each card to check.

1. Walk in an arc — DON'T stand still & spin.

Hint (Triangle) If you don't move, what is the triangle base length d?
👆 Click to reveal math reason
If d = 0, you CANNOT form a triangle! Without 2 viewpoints, AI cannot calculate depth (z).

2. Pause for walkers — DON'T film moving people.

Hint (Coords) Does a walking student stay at one (x, y, z) point?
👆 Click to reveal math reason
A moving person has different (x, y, z) in every frame! The math breaks and creates floating 'ghost blobs'.

3. Avoid shooting straight at glass or mirrors.

Hint (Light) What happens to a laser beam or reflection on glass?
👆 Click to reveal math reason
LiDAR: light passes through/bounces away → wrong t, wrong z. AI: reflections shift → fake (x, y, z) behind the wall.

4. Move at half speed — DON'T pan fast.

Hint (Matching) What does motion blur do to sharp wall corners?
👆 Click to reveal math reason
AI must match the EXACT same corner across frames. Blur hides corners → missing (x, y, z) dots (holes in the model).

5. Put a 1 m stick in the video.

Hint (Similar Δ) Do angles alone tell you SHAPE or REAL SIZE in meters?
👆 Click to reveal math reason
Angles only build SIMILAR triangles (right ratio, unknown size!). Without 1 real length, AI can't tell 30 cm from 6 m.

Main Entrance Mission: Divide & Conquer! (15 mins)

4 Fixed Roles
Job 1

Camera / Scanner Operator

Walks slowly in a smooth arc — waist height first, then angled slightly up to catch the roof & canopy.

Job 2

Safety Spotter

Walks beside the operator watching for steps, pillars, and passersby; calls out "Slow down!" when turning.

Job 3

Scale & Assumptions Checker

Places the 1 m meter stick clearly in frame; writes down interference (glare, glass, crowds) for our Assumptions Log.

Job 4

Empathy Observer

Watches real users (students, parents, staff, trolley/wheelchair users); notes problems & causes + takes 3–4 close-up photos.
